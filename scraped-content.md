### Treatments & Services

**General Dentistry (NHS + Private)**
- Full range for all age groups
- Regular examinations: comprehensive tooth/gum assessment + oral cancer screening
- NHS emergency: call before 9am / 111 out of hours

**Periodontal Treatment**
- Gum disease treatment — "a major cause of tooth loss"
- Treats loss of supporting bone and attachment tissues
- Meticulous oral hygiene emphasis
- Visiting periodontist services available

**Implant Dentistry**
- "One of the most satisfactory ways to replace missing natural teeth"
- Titanium post surgically placed in jawbone
- Can replace individual teeth or support bridges/dentures
- Free consultation available

**Cosmetic Dentistry**
- Teeth Whitening: Zoom Advanced Power & Enlighten Smiles systems
- Veneers
- Crowns
- White fillings / Dentine bonded restorations
- Dental bridges
- Gum reshaping
- Implants
- All cosmetic dentistry starts with well-maintained gums and bone support

**Orthodontics**
- Six Month Smiles (clear, non-metallic brace system) — Dr Ravi Pant is registered provider
- Invisalign clear aligners
- Conventional fixed braces
